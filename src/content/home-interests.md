**1) 3D Perception & Generation 🧠**

    \[3D Generation\]  Synthesizing 3D shapes or scenes from learned generative priors.

    **\[3D Reconstruction\]**  Recovering accurate 3D structure from limited visual evidence.

    **\[3D Scene Understanding\]**  Understanding 3D scenes by capturing geometry, semantics, and spatial structure.

**2) Robot Vision 🤖**

    **\[SLAM & Sensor Fusion\]**  Estimating robot state and mapping environments through multi-sensor integration.

    **\[3D Perception\]**  Understanding real-world environments through 3D geometric and semantic cues.

    **\[Collaborative Robot\]**  Enabling intelligent human-robot interaction through 3D perception and manipulation.
